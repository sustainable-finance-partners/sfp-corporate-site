2. Service description

2.1 What Phronesis is

Phronesis is the agentic economy's decision-assurance layer: a neutral substrate where agents and enterprises verify intent, price uncertainty, evaluate counterparties, coordinate transactions, and insure outcomes before acting. The atomic product is the Decision Asset (defined in Section 1) — a 10-field machine-readable, auditable decision packet that Customer can cite, reuse, dispute, and settle against. The Service is offered across the applied verticals published at phronesisintel.com/verticals, with V#0 AI Industrial Mobilization & Situational Awareness as the meta-vertical. The Service mints Decision Assets in response to Customer queries and persists them to a public-facing Calibration Scorecard (Market Memory) for verifiable accuracy tracking over time. Forecasting is the entry primitive, not the product.

2.2 What Phronesis is NOT

The Service does not constitute, and shall not be construed as:

  • Investment advice. Forecasts are probabilistic future-state predictions, not investment recommendations. Customers using forecasts for investment, trading, or capital allocation decisions do so at their own risk.
  • Legal, accounting, tax, or medical advice. Forecasts about regulatory, financial, or healthcare topics are informational projections, not professional advice. Customers should consult appropriately licensed professionals for such advice.
  • A guarantee of future events. Probabilistic forecasts express uncertainty; canonical-quantile p10/p50/p90 estimates are not predictions of actual outcomes. Past calibration accuracy does not guarantee future calibration accuracy.
  • Insurance, a guarantee, or an assumption of risk. Phronesis enables third parties to price, guarantee, or insure outcomes on the basis of the Record; it does not itself guarantee, underwrite, or insure any outcome, and it does not assume Customer's or any agent's obligations.
