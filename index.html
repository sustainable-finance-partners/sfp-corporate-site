What SFP is

Sustainable Finance Partners is the operating entity behind one product: Phronesis.

SFP is a Colorado limited liability company. It exists to operate Phronesis — the agentic economy's decision-assurance layer — and to do that with the credentials, contracts, and governance substrate that institutional capital allocators and federal counterparties can diligence as a discrete legal entity.

What SFP holds, as the parent operating entity:

  • Intellectual property — the Phronesis codebase, the Decision Asset schema, the six-primitive architecture, the V#0 meta-vertical, the Market Memory ledger.
  • Customer contracts — every per-call agreement, every subscription tier (Discovery / Inquiry / Practice / Practice+ / Enterprise), every Decision Asset settlement obligation.
  • Federal credentials — UEI E5FZM8GKGPJ6, EIN 99-0917951, SBC SBC_002676886, ORCID 0009-0000-5641-1456, NSF ID 0000A8RD8. The credentials that let SFP pursue SBIR / STTR / DOE / NSF / DOD opportunities and that any federal counterparty can verify independently.
  • Trademark filing — PHRONESIS, USPTO serial 99842940 (as-filed scope; the canonical trademark surface for the product brand).
  • Governance substrate — the operating model, the ratification ledger, the strategic canonical, the legal protections substrate, the runbooks. The discipline layer that makes the entity diligence-able.
  • Stewardship-mode purpose — SFP's posture is to allocate capital and operate the product in service of long-arc societal infrastructure. V#0 (AI Industrial Mobilization & Situational Awareness) is the meta-vertical the corporate entity cares about most.

What SFP is not.

SFP is not a separate forecasting platform, a separate consulting practice, or a separate advisory firm. There is one product — Phronesis — and SFP operates it. Earlier framing on this site described SFP as "a verified-correctness forecasting platform"; that was the pre-pivot brand state and has been superseded by the decision-assurance-layer canonical (ratified 2026-05-21 onward; full canonical at phronesisintel.com). Older framing is preserved via supersession-forward discipline, not retroactively deleted.

SFP is also not an investment advisor, a broker-dealer, or a fiduciary for customer capital decisions. Phronesis mints Decision Assets that capital allocators may consult; Decision Assets are not investment recommendations. The non-investment-advice posture is canonical at the legal-substrate layer and unchanged by this positioning recut.

What Phronesis is (compact)

Phronesis is the agentic economy's decision-assurance layer.

A neutral substrate where agents and enterprises verify intent, price uncertainty, evaluate counterparties, coordinate transactions, and insure outcomes before acting. Forecasting is the entry primitive, not the product. The atomic product is the Decision Asset — a 10-field machine-readable, auditable decision packet that an agent or enterprise can cite, reuse, dispute, and settle against. The compounding moat is Market Memory — the resolved-outcome ledger that strengthens on wall-clock time.

The Aristotelian name is deliberate. Phronesis — practical wisdom — is the form of wisdom that guides judgment in domains where rules cannot be mechanically applied. The product names that function precisely. The platform: phronesisintel.com. The full Decision Asset schema, the calibration scorecard, the API spec, the OpenAPI surface, the agent-discoverable manifests — all there.
